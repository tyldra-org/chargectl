Stop your MacBook charging at 80%.

Lithium-ion batteries wear faster when they sit at 100%. Chargectl stops charging at the limit you choose, from 20% to 100%, and your Mac runs on the charger while the battery rests.

brew install --cask tyldra-org/tools/chargectl

Or download the app from GitHub. Free and open source.

  • Apple silicon MacBook Air and MacBook Pro
  • macOS 27 or later
  • Apache-2.0

Holds your limit, even when the app is closed

Works with macOS’s own limit

Chargectl sets the charge limit macOS already has, so your limit shows in System Settings › Battery and holds while the Mac sleeps.

Goes below 80%

macOS’s own setting starts at 80%. Chargectl sets limits down to 20%, for a Mac that stays on the charger all day.

Leaves nothing behind

Uninstall restores normal charging and your macOS limit, and removes every file Chargectl added.

Set the limit, sailing and Full by

Drag the limit anywhere from 20% to 100%. Sailing lets the charge drift down a few percent before topping up again, so the battery isn’t charged all day. Full by charges to 100% just in time for a time you set, then goes back to your limit.

Watch battery health over months

One summary a day, kept for years: health, charge cycles, hours at 90% or more and the warmest moment, next to the last 24 hours of charge, temperature and power.

Widgets in every size

Charge, power, health, today, Full by, time left and quick controls on your desktop, plus Control Center controls for charge control, Top Up, Discharge and Full by.

Questions

How do I stop my MacBook charging at 80%?

Install Chargectl, open it, and pick 80% in the setup window, or later drag the white marker in its menu bar panel. When the battery reaches 80%, charging stops and your Mac runs on the charger until you unplug it.

macOS already has a charge limit. Why use Chargectl?

Recent macOS versions can limit charging from 80% to 100% in System Settings › Battery. Chargectl uses that same limit and adds limits down to 20%, sailing, Full by, heat protection, discharging to the limit, and a record of your battery’s health over months.

How is Chargectl different from AlDente?

Both stop a MacBook from charging to 100%. Chargectl is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 license, with no paid tier, and holds the limit through macOS’s own charge limit, so it keeps working while the Mac sleeps or the app is closed. Use one or the other: two charge limiters fight over the same settings.

Does it work on Intel Macs or older versions of macOS?

No. Chargectl needs an Apple silicon MacBook and macOS 27 or later.

Does Chargectl collect any data?

No. It has no account and no analytics. It contacts GitHub only to check for new releases, and you can turn that off in Settings › Updates.

How do I uninstall it?

Settings › Uninstall Chargectl restores normal charging and your macOS limit, and removes every file Chargectl added. Dragging the app to the Trash does the same within a few minutes.

Install Chargectl

brew install --cask tyldra-org/tools/chargectl

Or download the app from GitHub. Then open Chargectl from Applications; a short setup installs its helper and picks your limit.
